
LOAN PORTFOLIO & CREDIT RISK ANALYTICS
Portfolio Insights Report

IMPORTANT DATA NOTE
This project uses synthetic educational data. The results demonstrate
credit risk analysis techniques and do not represent actual Canadian
borrowers, lenders, or lending activity.

1. PORTFOLIO OVERVIEW

- Total loans analyzed: 5,000
- Total simulated defaults: 268
- Simulated default rate: 5.36%
- Total loan value: $98,147,350.90
- Average loan amount: $19,629.47
- Average credit score: 688.61
- Average annual income: $65,769.70
- Average debt-to-income ratio: 33.18%

2. CREDIT SCORE RISK

The below-600 credit score group has the highest simulated default
rate at 17.39%.

The 600-699 group has a simulated default rate of 6.38%.
The 700-749 group has a rate of 2.73%.
The 750-and-above group has the lowest rate at 1.26%.

INSIGHT:
In this synthetic dataset, lower credit scores are associated with
higher simulated default rates. Credit score bands could be useful
for further portfolio segmentation and risk analysis.

3. LOAN PURPOSE RISK

Small business loans have the highest simulated default rate at 10.00%.
Education loans follow at 7.61%.
Vehicle loans have the lowest rate among the listed purposes at 3.51%.

INSIGHT:
Simulated default rates vary by loan purpose. These differences could
be explored further alongside loan size, income, and credit score.

4. PROVINCIAL ANALYSIS

New Brunswick has a simulated default rate of 7.22%.
Saskatchewan follows at 6.57%.
Ontario has a rate of 5.41% across 1,961 loans.
Newfoundland and Labrador has the lowest rate at 3.13%, based on
64 loans.

INSIGHT:
Provincial results vary, but sample sizes differ substantially.
Rates from smaller groups should be interpreted cautiously and
should not be treated as evidence about real provincial borrowers.

5. RECOMMENDED POWER BI VISUALS

- KPI cards: total loans, simulated defaults, simulated default rate,
  and total loan value.
- Bar chart: simulated default rate by credit score group.
- Bar chart: simulated default rate by loan purpose.
- Bar chart: simulated default rate by province.
- Table: loan counts, defaults, and simulated default rates.
- Scatter plot: credit score versus loan amount, with default status
  used to distinguish records.

6. CONCLUSION

The SQL analysis identifies patterns across credit score groups,
loan purposes, and provinces in the synthetic dataset. The results
provide a foundation for an interactive Power BI dashboard and
demonstrate a workflow using Python, SQLite, SQL, and Excel.

All findings are descriptive and based on simulated data. They
should not be interpreted as causal relationships or real-world
credit risk estimates.
